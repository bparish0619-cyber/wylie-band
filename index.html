Who We Are

ABOUT THE PROGRAM

Honor Band

Conducted by Mr. McDonald. 5A Varsity ensemble. Requires passing grades, full attendance, All-Region participation, and Varsity performance standards.

Symphonic & Concert Band

Symphonic Band (Ms. Ray) competes 5A Non-Varsity. Concert Band is 5A Sub Non-Varsity, focused on developing musicality. Both require passing grades and full attendance.

Jazz, Steel & Guard

Jazz and Steel Band meet 3rd period daily. Color Guard integrates with the Marching Band in the fall and operates as Winter Guard independently in the spring.
